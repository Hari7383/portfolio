Image Retexturing using AI

02CASE STUDY / PROCESS

Industrial and architectural teams spend a lot of manual effort getting CAD renders to look presentation-ready. This project explores whether an image-to-image GAN can retexture those renders automatically, so drafting output reaches a consistent visual quality faster.

Approach

  • Framed retexturing as an image-to-image translation problem and trained a Pix2Pix conditional GAN on paired input and target images.
  • Prepared and preprocessed the paired dataset, then tuned the generator and discriminator for texture fidelity.
  • Iterated on outputs visually against the source renders to check that geometry stayed intact while surface texture changed.

Where it applies

  • CAD and drafting workflows in manufacturing, architecture and oil & gas, where visual quality matters.
  • Asset and concept visualization for automotive, gaming and VFX pipelines.

Skills demonstrated

  • Generative modelling with GANs and deep learning for computer vision.
  • Dataset preparation, training and optimization for a real-world use case.

Next steps

  • Add quantitative evaluation (for example SSIM or FID) alongside visual review.
  • Train at higher resolution and integrate the model into an automated CAD rendering pipeline.
